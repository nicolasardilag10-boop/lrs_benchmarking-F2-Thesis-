1    Introduction

Long Reads has developed the High Fidelity (HiFi) reads from Pacific Biosciences with lengths
from 10-25 kbp with an accuracy of 99.95%. The most recent released is the R10 Chemistry
of Oxford Nanopore Technologies where 882kpbs and accuracy over 99% is achieved. This
allow them to be useful to resolve complex and large structural variants [4].
Sequence alignment is a critical step during downstream analysis in genomic research and
clinical diagnostics. Its accuracy is required to guarantee adequate variant detection, genome
assembly, personalized medicine, and comparative genomics [2].
Minimap implements a seed-chain-align approach, where minimizers are used as seeds to
recognize potential alignments quickly [3]
Sequence alignment is a critical step during the downstream analysis in genomic research
clinical diagnostics. Its accuracy is require to guarantee adequate variant detection, genome
assembly, personalized medicine and comparative genomics [3]
Structural variants are alterations >50 bp described to be one of the most effect drivers in
genetic variation. Their detection require high alignment accuracy to identify duplications,
inversions, translocations and complex cluster rearrangements. Their inadequate detection
might represent a genomic barrier into understanding genetic variation and their role in the
patient diseases [2].
VACmap has a main differences with the reference and read sequences due to it heavily
penalize splits when searching for chains causing that in complex SV as duplication, inversion,
translocations or cluster combinations to being not considered [2].
Vacmap on the other hand include the matches as quadruple called anchors, where start
position of long read and references sequences, strand match and anchors length are included.
They order the end positions of long read. Then the non linear chaining algorithm promotes
extension of the chain to subsequent anchors which preserved linear relationship with the
proceding anchor, enhance connection with a positive score. It penalize extension to anchors
which disrupt linearity by assesing negative score to such conections. So the optimal non/linear
alignment ist the chain with the highest aggregate score (the longest path). To determine
each linear subalignet this non/linear alignment can be subdived to a non-linear junction,
eliminating the traditional neccesity of post alingment steps to reconstruct the rearrangament
from a poll of error prone independent subalignments [2].
Implementation of long/read sequencing analysis are commonly implemented specially in the
genome regions with tandem repeats where SV are predominantly observed. However long
reads, has the challenge has high sequencing errors due to their length [2].
In the past it was implemented always algorithm with a reference genome where subalignments
are doing using a see-chain-approach call as minimizers. This consist on seeds or matches of
k-mers with the genome reference, which are co-linear selected to form chains, then those are
extenden bidirectionally until finding significant differences from the reads and the reference
